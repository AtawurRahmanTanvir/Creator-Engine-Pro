🏗️ AI Roles & Architecture

This application uses a strict Tag Routing System. The AIs have specific rules on who they can communicate with:

  • @administrator: The Custom GPT Orchestrator. It manages tasks, decides which worker to use, and waits for your Dispatch approval.
  • Worker AIs (@chatgpt, @gemini, etc): They do the hard work. They can ONLY communicate back to the @administrator.
  • @final: The Editor. It reviews the Administrator's compiled data, formats it perfectly, and outputs it to you.

🎙️ "System Recording" Switch

To prevent AIs from talking to each other prematurely, use this switch:

  • 🔴 OFF (Warm-up Mode): The Master Router pauses. Your messages are sent directly to the AI, but responses are ignored by the router. Use this to prepare the environment.
  • 🟢 ON (Active Routing): The Orchestration loop starts. Tags (e.g., @chatgpt) will instantly route data between workers.

🧠 Starting a Workflow (v1.1.0)

  1. Turn System Recording OFF.
  2. Select Final AI and send its formatting rules (e.g., "Act as the Editor. Always format in Markdown..."). Wait for it to confirm.
  3. Select Administrator. Since it is a pre-trained Custom GPT, it already knows its role! Just ask "Are you ready?" and ensure it replies in the strict Planning Mode format.
  4. Turn System Recording ON.
  5. Give your actual project to the Administrator. It will plan the steps and wait for your explicit "Dispatch" command before routing any data!
